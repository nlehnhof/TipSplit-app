TipSplit Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

TipSplit ("the app") is a tip-splitting calculator for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

No account required

TipSplit does not require you to create an account or sign in to use the calculator or any of its features.

What data the app stores

The app stores the following information locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and we do not have access to it:

  • Worker names and roles you enter, for tip splitting and (if you use the Pro tier) saved workers and teams
  • Calculation inputs and results (tip totals, split method, per-worker amounts), including your calculation history if you use the Pro tier
  • Your app preferences and onboarding status

This data stays on your device unless you delete the app or clear its storage. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Subscriptions and payment

TipSplit Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription. Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms. TipSplit does not collect, see, or store your payment card details. We use RevenueCat, a subscription management service, to validate purchase receipts and manage entitlement status; RevenueCat receives anonymized purchase/transaction data necessary to confirm your subscription is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics and tracking

TipSplit does not currently use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Children's privacy

TipSplit is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
