TipSplit Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

TipSplit ("the app") is a restaurant tip-splitting calculator. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices. TipSplit is built to work fully offline, and the basic calculator does not require creating an account.

Information stored on your device

TipSplit stores the following data locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage:

  • Your current in-progress calculation (total tips, method, worker list), so it's restored if you close and reopen the app
  • Saved Workers and Saved Teams you create, for reuse across calculations
  • A history of past calculations (capped at 100 entries)

None of this data is transmitted to us, uploaded to a server, or shared with any third party. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data permanently.

Subscription purchases (TipSplit Pro)

TipSplit Pro, an optional paid subscription, is processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store (whichever you install the app from) and managed using RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. When you purchase or restore a subscription, Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process transaction and entitlement information (such as your purchase status and an anonymous subscriber identifier) to verify your subscription and keep it active across devices. TipSplit does not receive or store your payment details — those are handled entirely by Apple/Google.

What TipSplit does not do

  • No account creation or sign-in is required to use the calculator
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included in the app
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access is requested
  • No data is sold or shared with advertisers

Children's privacy

TipSplit is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
